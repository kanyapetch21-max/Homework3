Question 3 (b): Are the customer reservations satisfied at every location?
Explain.
======================================================
No. Total reservations = 21, but only 18 bikes can be delivered.

This shortfall is caused by a transportation-capacity bottleneck
(min cut = 18), not a lack of total supply (total supply = 22 is greater
than total demand = 21).

Reservations are NOT satisfied at every location:
  - Location D is short by 2 bikes (receives 8 of 10 needed).
  - Location E is short by 1 bike (receives 10 of 11 needed).
